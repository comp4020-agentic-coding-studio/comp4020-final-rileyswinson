# Recordings

The toilet's sounds are real recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org),
every one released by its author under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain).
Each was trimmed, mixed to mono, brought to a common level and re-encoded as
MP3. Nothing here requires credit; this is so the sources can be found.
