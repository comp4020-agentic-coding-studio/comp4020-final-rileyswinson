# Crit 10

I note I did not attend this crit; instead, I presented this in the make-up crit in week 11.

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I sent my app in a meidum-sized group chat to see what people did with it. I saw my friends spend, cumulativley, around 15 hours painting random things. Sometimes they would claim who made what -- and sometimes, it would be left entierly anonymous. The amount of fun they had, entierly volunetarily, with my little app showed that I had vibecoded something which, in some way, metaphorically spoke to them. They send me a good amount of feedback, too. A couple of bugs, and a few suggestions in regards to interactivity, which is how I came up with the sticker system.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

Not much. I already knew how I like to collect feedback, and followed the same process I had in the past. And -- shocking -- I would love to make things which create fun for others.
