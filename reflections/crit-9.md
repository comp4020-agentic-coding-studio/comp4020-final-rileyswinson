# Crit 9

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I felt sick for some time, but i had a good idea of what i wanted this project to be. it initially stemmed from a somewhat disingenous "how can I make MS Paint my final project" which then turned into a discussion which turned into the idea of the expression of self through vandalism of bathroom stalls and how the primitivism of a bit of art is not necessarily a statement against its quality -- and this leads into how one defines "good" etc etc. 

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

Well, it made me think of the kind of experience I want users to have with what I make. I know that i mainly like on the technical side of things, but i would love to make things that my friends and strangers use and get enjoymet from. Showing this, albeit AI, little web-game with my friends resulted in a good chunk of fun. And i like that.
